---
description: Meeting minutes from a recording or a transcript in your Scriptivox library
argument-hint: <recording link, or words from the transcript name>
---

Use the meeting-minutes skill on "$ARGUMENTS".

If it is a link, transcribe it with Scriptivox first. Otherwise find the transcript with `search_transcripts`, read every page of `get_transcript`, and write the minutes: summary, decisions, action items with owner and due date, open questions.
